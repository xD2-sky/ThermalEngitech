Shop Floor Equipment

Real machine specifications documented from the facility's own equipment register.

Welding

MIG/TIG/ERW welding processes. Rectifiers rated 400 Amps. Argon Arc welding set with suppressor (TIG). CO2 welding set, 600 Amps. Dedicated stainless-steel pipe welding unit.

Plate Bending

Pyramid-type plate bending machine. Minimum diameter 700mm. Length up to 2500mm. Thickness up to 32mm, in-house.

Cutting

Mechanically operated shearing up to 6mm. Power-operated hacksaw. Flame cutting up to 150mm thickness. CNC plasma profile cutting.

Tube Bending

Job length up to 2m, job size up to 500mm diameter. Bend saw for angle & channel sections up to 12mm thick. Carbon arc gouging, abrasive cutting.

Machining Facility

Radial drills, boring machines, and plano milling. 3-roll plate bending machines, hydraulic bend press and shears.

Portable Tools

Hand grinders, polishers, and cut-off machines. Pneumatic grinders and drills. Tube extractors and tube expanders with controllers.

Material Handling

Shop equipped with electrically operated overhead EOT cranes for heavy shell and tube-bundle movement.

Testing Equipment

Hydraulic testing machine rated up to 200 kg/cm². Pneumatic testing machine rated up to 100 PSI.

Surface Finish & Painting

Sand blasting and spray painting, with an iron-oxide coating line. 3-phase power connection with 160 KVA / 415V diesel generator backup for power failure.
