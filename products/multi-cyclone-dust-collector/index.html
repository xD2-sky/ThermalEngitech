Multi Cyclone Dust Collector

"High-Efficiency Centrifugal Particulate Separation Block"

High Resolution Schematic CAD Layout

Design & Operations Overview

An essential pre-cleaning device for solid fuel boilers. Relies on centrifugal forces developed within multiple small swirl cyclone cones to separate fly-ash and charcoal particles from exhaust stack flue gases before venting.

Technical Specifications

Collection EfficiencyUp to 85% for particles larger than 15 microns
Vane Cone MaterialAbrasive-resistant alloy Cast Iron (Chilled CI)
Operational Draft DropSlight gas friction pressure drop of 40-75 mm WC
Exhaust Flow Capacity1,500 M³/hr to 45,000 M³/hr
Base Mounting SupportHeavy channel steel structure with ash drop bin

Key Performance Features

Consists of multiple high-durability Grade cast iron swirl vane cones
Removes up to 85% of coarse particles, reducing wear-tear on downstream wet scrubbers
Completely passive structure with zero moving mechanical components to wear out
Equipped with airtight rotary air-lock valve to ensure zero air bypass
