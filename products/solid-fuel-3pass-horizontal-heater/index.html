Wood/Coal Fired 3-Pass Horizontal Thermic Fluid Heater

Design & Operations Overview

An eco-friendly and highly economical process heating system utilizing firewood or charcoal briquettes. Constructed using heavy-duty seamless coils and a spacious combustion chamber to maximize radiant transfer, reducing operational heating expenditure by up to 50% compared to oil heating.

Technical Specifications

Heat Capacity2,00,000 kcal/hr to 30,00,000 kcal/hr
Maximum Temperatures280°C to 300°C
Solid Fuel OptionsWood logs, Steam Coal, Dry Biomass Briquettes, Coconut shell
Draft AssemblyBalanced draft (Both primary FD Fan & robust ID blower)
Structure SizingHorizontal skidded setup with easy ash cleanout ports

Key Performance Features

Inhouse fabricated thick-walled seamless coiled tubes to ASTM A106 Gr. B specifications
Spacious furnace box provides optimal fuel residence time and clean flue pathing
Fitted with dual high-discharge primary thermic fluid circulating pumps
Includes air pre-heater to return hot exit exhaust air back into combustion stack
