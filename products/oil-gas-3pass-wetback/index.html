Design & Operations Overview

An industry standard for clean process steam. Features a fully submerged wet-back turnaround chamber configuration to completely avoid dry-back thermal stresses and combustion bypass. Offers premium fuel economy and high dry steam fraction.

Technical Specifications

Capacity Range0.5 Ton/hr to 20.0 Tons/hr (500 to 20,000 kg/hr)
Operating PressureUp to 25.0 kg/cm²
Compatible FuelsLight Diesel Oil (LDO), HSD, Natural Gas, LPG, Furnace Oil, Dual Fuel
Steam TemperatureUp to 225°C
Thermal Efficiency88% (Up to 94% with external economizer & preheater)
Compliance CodeIBR 1950 Rules (Form VI) & ASME Sec I

Key Performance Features

Maximum heat transfer via extensive three-pass convective tubes
Integrated corrugated Morrison furnace to absorb severe stress expansions
Compatible with leading imported burners (Riello, Oilon, Bentley)
High insulation efficiency with pre-fitted 100mm high-density mineral wool
